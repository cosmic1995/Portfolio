Overview


Lowe's Pro Central is a unified enterprise management hub designed specifically for building contractors and professional buyers. Prior to this portal, contractors had to use five separate tools to track job orders, manage inventory, coordinate logistics, and check in-store pickups.

I led user research with 30+ Pro customers and designed "Pro Central," bringing invoicing, materials tracking, real-time inventory, and job progress reports into a single, cohesive dashboard layout.

The Challenge

Professional builders manage complex business cycles under tight timelines. They previously had to log in to separate legacy systems for checking store pickup orders, tracking delivery trucks, coordinating invoices, and checking stock availability. This fragmented flow caused coordination errors, ordering mistakes, and wasted hours for busy contractors.

The objective was to consolidate these five fragmented legacy tools into a unified, responsive dashboard experience optimized for high-intensity commercial project management workflows.

The Solution

I led the UX strategy and product design for the consolidated portal:

Research-Driven Architecture: Conducted field research with 30+ Pro customers and contractors to identify 12 key pain points and workflow overlaps.

Unified Dashboards: Designed modular widget frameworks so users can monitor orders, inventory, and invoices in one interface.

In-Store Pickups & Staging: Developed real-time pickup barcode trackers and delivery ETA widgets for on-site managers.
