The Challenge

The oncology platformâ€™s original clinical interface was dense, confusing, and relied on legacy paper-form logic. Medical providers struggled to configure genomic tests accurately, and the interface lacked support for keyboard-only navigation or clear screen-reader labeling. These design flaws caused ordering errors, delayed patient screenings, and lowered platform engagement.

The objective was to rebuild the ordering flow using progressive disclosure, modern input structures, and full compliance with WCAG 2.1 AA web standards.

The Solution

I led the redesign of the onboarding and test-ordering workflow:

Progressive Disclosure Wizards: Broke complex clinical forms into distinct, digestible steps (Patient Info -> Test Selection -> Clinical Details), reducing cognitive load.

Accessible System Architecture: Standardized focus rings, color contrast ratios (minimum 4.5:1), and explicit ARIA labels across the interface.

Rigorous Usability Testing: Conducted cognitive walk-throughs with healthcare coordinators to refine error handling and validation logic.

The Result


50% Engagement Lift

Simplified patient and test onboarding forms significantly boosted successful online orders by clinic staff.

Full WCAG Compliance

System audit confirmed 100% compliance with WCAG 2.1 accessibility standards across all screen readers.

Zero Form Errors

In-context validation and clear error states minimized incorrect orders, speed up approvals.
