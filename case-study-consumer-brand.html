At Omnicom Media Group (Annalect India), I established our first in-house UX practice. I led the design, journey mapping, and prototyping of interactive digital brand portals for global consumer accounts.

The Challenge

Global brands require extremely premium, interactive digital interfaces to engage customers and protect their brand image. Our teams previously relied on external agencies to prototype brand experiences, causing communication bottlenecks, slow design turnarounds, and inconsistent design handoffs.

The objective was to establish an in-house UX practice capable of delivering rapid, high-fidelity prototypes and standardized UI libraries directly to our global brand partners.

The Solution

I built the agency's first in-house UX pipeline:

In-House Capabilities: Defined user research frameworks, component standards, and high-fidelity prototyping protocols.

Immersive Prototypes: Created interactive design systems for BMW customization flows, McDonald's loyalty loops, and Sony Pictures portal rollouts.

Stakeholder Alignment: Presented interactive mockups and design rationale to groups of 60+ global stakeholders, shortening alignment cycles.

The Result


40% Lower Agency Spend

Bringing prototyping and UI design in-house eliminated third-party design agency dependencies.

60+ Stakeholders Aligned

High-fidelity, interactive prototypes made user testing and stakeholder sign-off straightforward.

2 Weeks Saved

Consistent UI specifications and direct code handoffs reduced delivery time for development sprints.
